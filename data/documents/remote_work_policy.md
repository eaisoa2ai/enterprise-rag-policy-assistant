# Remote Work Policy

Northwind Group operates a hybrid model: employees are expected in the
office at least 2 days per week, with the remaining days eligible for
remote work, subject to manager approval.

## Eligibility

Roles requiring physical presence (facilities, on-site security, lab
equipment operation) are excluded from remote eligibility; check with your
manager if you're unsure which category your role falls into.

## Fully Remote Arrangements

Employees may request a fully remote arrangement (0 required office days)
after 12 months of tenure, subject to manager and HR approval. Fully remote
employees must be available during core hours (10:00 AM–3:00 PM in their
team's primary time zone) for meetings.

## Equipment

Northwind Group provides a laptop, monitor, and a one-time $300 home-office
stipend for remote employees. Equipment remains company property and must
be returned upon departure.

## International Remote Work

Working remotely from a country other than your employment country requires
prior approval from HR and Legal, due to tax and employment-law
implications. Unapproved international remote work can create tax
obligations for the company and is treated as a policy violation.

## Security Expectations While Remote

Remote employees must connect to company systems only over the corporate
VPN and must not work from unsecured public Wi-Fi without VPN enabled. See
the Acceptable Use Policy for full device and network security
requirements.
