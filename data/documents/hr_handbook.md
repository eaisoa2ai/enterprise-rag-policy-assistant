# Employee Handbook

Welcome to Northwind Group. This handbook covers the policies that apply to
every employee, regardless of department.

## Leave and PTO

Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of paid time off (PTO) per month,
totaling 21 days per year. PTO accrual begins on your first day and is
prorated for partial months. Unused PTO carries over up to a maximum of 5
days into the next calendar year; anything beyond that is forfeited unless
your local jurisdiction requires otherwise.

Sick leave is separate from PTO: employees receive 10 paid sick days per
year, which do not carry over. A doctor's note is required for sick leave
lasting more than 3 consecutive days.

Parental leave is 16 weeks paid for the primary caregiver and 6 weeks paid
for the secondary caregiver, available to all employees regardless of
tenure.

## Working Hours

Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM local time, Monday through
Friday, with a 30-minute unpaid lunch break. Flexible start times between
7:30 AM and 10:00 AM are permitted with manager approval.

## Onboarding

New hires complete a 5-day onboarding program covering company systems,
benefits enrollment, and a meeting with their assigned mentor. Benefits
elections must be completed within 30 days of the start date.

## Performance Reviews

Formal performance reviews happen twice a year, in June and December.
Managers are expected to hold informal 1:1 check-ins at least monthly.

## Contacting HR

General HR questions go to hr-support@northwindgroup.example. For
confidential concerns about a manager or team member, contact HR directly
rather than raising it in a team channel.
