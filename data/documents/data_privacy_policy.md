# Data Privacy Policy

This policy governs how Northwind Group collects, stores, and processes
personal data, and applies jointly to Legal and IT Security.

## Data Classification

Personal data is classified into 3 tiers: Tier 1 (name, email — low
sensitivity), Tier 2 (financial or health information — high sensitivity),
and Tier 3 (government ID numbers, biometric data — restricted). Tier 3
data requires encryption at rest and in transit with no exceptions, and
access is limited to a named list of approved systems.

## Data Subject Requests

Under GDPR and similar regulations, individuals can request access to,
correction of, or deletion of their personal data. Northwind Group commits
to responding to a verified data subject request within 30 calendar days.
Deletion requests are routed through Legal, who confirm no legal hold
applies before IT executes the deletion.

## Data Retention

Customer data is retained for 7 years after the last active transaction
for tax and audit purposes, then automatically purged unless a legal hold
is in effect. Employee data is retained for 7 years after termination.

## Cross-Border Data Transfer

Transferring Tier 2 or Tier 3 data outside the country of collection
requires a Standard Contractual Clause (SCC) on file with Legal before the
transfer occurs — this applies to new vendor integrations as much as
internal systems.

## Breach Notification Timelines

A confirmed breach involving Tier 2 or Tier 3 data must be reported to the
relevant data protection authority within 72 hours of confirmation, per
GDPR Article 33. Legal owns this notification; IT Security owns the
technical containment and evidence preservation feeding into it.
