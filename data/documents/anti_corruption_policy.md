# Anti-Corruption and Anti-Bribery Policy

This policy implements Northwind Group's obligations under anti-bribery
laws including the FCPA and UK Bribery Act, and is administered by Legal.

## Gifts and Hospitality

Gifts to or from a government official are prohibited outright, regardless
of value. Gifts to or from a commercial business partner are permitted up
to $100 in value per occasion, must be logged in the gifts register, and
must never be cash or a cash equivalent (gift cards included).

## Facilitation Payments

Facilitation payments — small payments to expedite a routine government
action — are prohibited without exception, even in jurisdictions where
they are locally considered customary.

## Third-Party Intermediaries

Any agent, consultant, or intermediary engaging with a government official
on Northwind Group's behalf must complete enhanced anti-corruption due
diligence before engagement, and their compensation must not include
success fees contingent on a specific government approval being granted.

## Political Contributions

Corporate political contributions require CFO and Legal pre-approval and
must be publicly disclosed per local law regardless of amount.

## Whistleblower Protection

Reports made in good faith through the ethics hotline regarding suspected
bribery or corruption are protected from retaliation, and Legal
investigates every report regardless of the reporter's seniority or the
seniority of the person implicated.

## Sanctions Screening

All new vendors and customers in restricted or high-risk jurisdictions are
screened against sanctions lists before onboarding; a positive match blocks
onboarding pending Legal review.
