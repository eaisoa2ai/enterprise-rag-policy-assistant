# Acceptable Use Policy

This policy covers how every employee is expected to use Northwind Group
devices, accounts, and networks.

## Passwords and Authentication

Passwords must be at least 14 characters and unique to each system —
password reuse across personal and company accounts is prohibited.
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, VPN, and any
system containing customer data.

## Company Devices

Company laptops must have disk encryption and the endpoint security agent
active at all times; disabling either is a policy violation. Personal use
of a company device (checking personal email, browsing) is permitted in
moderation but must never involve installing unapproved software.

## Data Handling

Customer data and internal documents must not be copied to personal cloud
storage (personal Google Drive, Dropbox, iCloud) or personal USB drives.
Use only company-approved storage and sharing tools.

## Email and Phishing

Employees are expected to report suspected phishing emails using the
"Report Phishing" button rather than forwarding them. Northwind Group runs
periodic phishing simulation tests; repeated failures may require
additional security training.

## Reporting a Security Incident

Any suspected security incident — lost device, suspicious account
activity, a successful phishing click — must be reported to IT Security
within 24 hours via security@northwindgroup.example or the incident
hotline. Prompt reporting is not a disciplinary matter; delayed reporting
of a known incident is treated more seriously than the incident itself.

Detailed incident response procedures for the security team are covered in
the IT Security Policy.
