# Procurement and Vendor Policy

This policy governs how Finance approves and manages vendor spend above
standard expense-report thresholds.

## Purchase Order Thresholds

Purchases between $2,000 and $25,000 require a purchase order (PO)
approved by the department director and a Finance business partner.
Purchases over $25,000 require VP Finance approval and a minimum of two
competing vendor quotes on file.

## Vendor Onboarding

New vendors must complete a financial and security due-diligence review
before a PO can be issued. Vendors handling customer data additionally
require sign-off from IT Security and Legal, per the Data Privacy Policy.
Onboarding typically takes 5–10 business days; it cannot be expedited for
purchases initiated without a PO already in progress.

## Contract Terms

Standard payment terms are net-60. Any vendor contract requesting payment
terms shorter than net-30, or an upfront deposit over 25% of contract
value, requires VP Finance sign-off regardless of the total contract
amount.

## Sole-Source Purchases

Purchases made without competing quotes (sole-source) over $10,000 require
a written justification and CFO approval, logged in the procurement system
for audit purposes.

## Recurring Spend Review

All vendor contracts over $50,000 annually are reviewed at renewal by
Finance to confirm continued business need before auto-renewal is allowed
to proceed.
