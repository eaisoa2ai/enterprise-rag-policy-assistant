# Expense and Travel Policy

## Booking Travel

All flights and hotels must be booked through the corporate travel portal.
Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours; business class is
permitted for flights over 6 hours with manager pre-approval.

## Expense Limits

Meals while traveling are reimbursed up to $75/day domestic and $100/day
international. Client entertainment is reimbursable up to $150 per person
with a business justification noted in the expense report. Alcohol is
reimbursable only as part of a client meal, not for personal consumption.

## Submitting Expenses

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being
incurred; reports submitted after 60 days require VP approval regardless of
amount. All expenses over $25 require an itemized receipt — a credit card
statement alone is not sufficient.

## Approval Thresholds

Expenses under $500 are approved by your direct manager. Expenses between
$500 and $2,000 require director-level approval. Anything over $2,000 goes
through the Procurement and Vendor Policy process rather than standard
expense reporting.

## Non-Reimbursable Items

Personal entertainment, traffic or parking fines, and travel companion
costs (a spouse or family member accompanying a business trip) are not
reimbursable.
