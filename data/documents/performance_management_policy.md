# Performance Management and Disciplinary Policy

This policy is administered by HR and covers performance improvement and
disciplinary procedures beyond the standard review cycle described in the
Employee Handbook.

## Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs)

A PIP is initiated when a manager documents sustained underperformance
across at least 2 consecutive review cycles, or a single serious
performance failure. PIPs run for 60 or 90 days depending on role
seniority, with a written check-in at the 30-day mark. HR must review and
approve every PIP before it's issued to the employee.

## Disciplinary Tiers

Disciplinary action follows 4 tiers: verbal warning, written warning, final
written warning, and termination. A tier may be skipped for serious
misconduct (safety violations, harassment, Code of Conduct violations
involving dishonesty) with HR and Legal sign-off.

## Investigations

Any complaint alleging harassment, discrimination, or Code of Conduct
violation triggers a formal HR investigation, conducted independently of
the reporting employee's management chain. Investigations target
completion within 30 days; the complainant and respondent are both
informed of the outcome, though full investigation details remain
confidential.

## Termination Approval

Involuntary termination requires sign-off from HR and the department VP
regardless of tier reached. Terminations related to a harassment or
discrimination investigation additionally require Legal sign-off before
being finalized.

## Appeals

An employee may appeal a disciplinary decision once, in writing, to HR
within 10 business days of the decision. HR assigns a reviewer who was not
involved in the original decision.
