# Code of Conduct

Every Northwind Group employee is expected to act with honesty, fairness,
and respect in all business dealings.

## Core Principles

- Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect regardless of
  role, background, or seniority.
- Make business decisions based on merit, not personal relationships.
- Never ask a colleague to do something you wouldn't be comfortable
  explaining to your manager or to a regulator.
- Report suspected violations promptly — see Reporting below.

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any outside financial interest, board membership,
or family relationship that could reasonably be seen to influence a
business decision they're involved in. Disclosures go to your manager and
are logged with HR.

## Respectful Workplace

Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation of any kind are prohibited.
This applies to in-person interactions, video calls, and written
communication including chat and email.

## Reporting a Concern

Employees can report a suspected Code of Conduct violation to their
manager, HR, or through the anonymous ethics hotline at
ethics-hotline.northwindgroup.example. Retaliation against anyone who
reports a good-faith concern is itself a violation of this code and will
be treated as a serious disciplinary matter.

## Scope

This Code applies to all employees, contractors, and officers of Northwind
Group globally. Department-specific policies (financial controls, data
handling, security) provide more detail for roles where those risks are
higher — this document sets the baseline that applies to everyone.
